SECTION 1 — PLATFORM OVERVIEW 1. Platform OverviewThis document defines the complete requirements for building a multi-tenant Hospital Management SaaS platform. The platform is based on an existing single-hospital PHP system (AEH — Aakash Eye Hospital, Visnagar, Gujarat) and converts it into a scalable, professional SaaS product that can serve multiple hospitals simultaneously.
